About this project

Built to be useful in the worst five minutes of someone's day.

Disaster Resource Finder is a self-contained reference tool: emergency checklists, first aid steps, shelter information, and contact numbers, organized so they're fast to find under pressure. Everything runs client-side — your checklists, backpack, and profile stay on your device.

It's designed as a demonstration of what a calm, accessible emergency-preparedness product could look like — not a replacement for official alerts from your local disaster management authority, which you should always follow first.

Local-first

Data lives in your browser's storage.

Offline-ready

Core content works without a connection.

Accessible

High contrast, dyslexia-friendly, keyboard-first.

Private

No accounts, no tracking, no backend.
